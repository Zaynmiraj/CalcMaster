### Full Description (Formatted for Google Play Store)
```text
NoteCalc Pro is a next-generation smart notepad and reactive calculation engine. It combines the freedom of a note-taking app with the mathematical power of a financial spreadsheet and scientific calculator.

Write words and numbers naturally. Link lines dynamically. Reference previous subtotals. Watch your entire document update in real time whenever you change a single number.

Designed from the ground up to be 100% offline-first, NoteCalc Pro keeps all your calculations, notes, and financial data strictly private on your device.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✦ WHY NOTECALC PRO?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

▶ SMART NOTEPAD MEETS LIVE MATH
Type expressions naturally like "5 coffees at $4.50 = $22.50" or "Monthly Rent: 1200 + Utilities: 180". NoteCalc Pro understands human text, variables, and units, automatically evaluating results as you type.

▶ REACTIVE DAG LINE REFERENCING
Connect lines dynamically! Reference previous lines with ease (e.g., "Line 1 - Line 4" or "@Subtotal * 15%"). Modify any number at the top of your document, and every dependent line, subtotal, and tax calculation updates instantaneously with zero lag.

▶ VOICE MATH DICTATION
Hands full? Tap the microphone and speak your calculations naturally: "Fifty dollars to euros plus twenty percent". The built-in voice engine parses equations and currency conversions in real-time.

▶ PRIVATE AI FINANCIAL INTELLIGENCE
Transform complex calculation notes into executive financial audits and budget summaries with a single tap. NoteCalc Pro analyzes spending breakdowns, net balances, and budget health with zero account tracking.

▶ DUAL MODE: SMART NOTEPAD & PRO KEYPAD
Switch seamlessly between the open-ended Smart Notepad canvas and a precision Scientific & Standard Calculator keypad featuring memory registers (MC, M+, M-, MR), trigonometry, logarithms, and haptic feedback.

▶ PERPETUAL CALCULATION LEDGER
Never lose a calculation. The automatic perpetual calculation tape records every solved equation with timestamped receipts, instant re-insert, and exportable history.

▶ GLOBAL CURRENCY & UNIT CONVERSIONS
Convert over 150+ world currencies with up-to-date exchange rates, plus comprehensive unit conversions for length, mass, temperature, data, and time.

▶ MULTI-LANGUAGE & RTL SUPPORT
Full internationalization support including English, Arabic (with full right-to-left UI layouts), and Bengali.

▶ 100% OFFLINE-FIRST & PRIVATE
• No accounts or sign-ups required.
• No cloud storage of your financial calculations.
• No third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.
• Export your full document archive as JSON or formatted text anytime.

Whether you are calculating monthly budgets, planning project invoices, tracking freelance expenses, or solving engineering equations, NoteCalc Pro provides the fastest, most intuitive calculation experience on Android.

Download NoteCalc Pro today and experience mathematics that feels like thought.
```
